Thank you for visiting Youth Livelihoods Reads, an internal knowledge sharing space on youth livelihoods that helps the UNICEF team track and share key knowledge products from diverse sources. If you find an interesting read worth sharing with your colleagues, please drop the hyperlink here. Inputs collected through this form will help the MERL team curate a shared reading list hosted on GitHub Pages, to be refreshed monthly.
About this repository

This is an internal knowledge monitoring space for the GenU team which helps the tracking of updated news, knowledge, and evidence in the youth livelihoods space.

How it works going forward: The MERL team scans available sources and collects inputs from GenU colleagues to curate the monthly list, then the page will be updated accordingly. This is not a newsletter or a new product. It is a shared reading list of what GenU is tracking.
